# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep discussions focused on the work.
Harassment or discriminatory behaviour of any kind is not tolerated.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the contact details on
[numanulhaq.com](https://www.numanulhaq.com). Reports are handled confidentially.
